# Sexual Harassment of Women at Workplace (POSH) Act, 2013

### 1. Protection of Women in Educational Institutions
* **Coverage**: Under Section 2(o), the definition of "workplace" explicitly includes **higher educational institutions, colleges, schools, universities, and vocational centers**.
* **Protected Persons**: Protects all women students, researchers, trainees, interns, contractual staff, visitors, and regular employees.

### 2. Definition of Sexual Harassment (Section 2(n))
Sexual harassment includes any one or more of the following unwelcome acts or behavior (directly or by implication):
* Physical contact and advances.
* A demand or request for sexual favors.
* Making sexually colored remarks or comments.
* Showing pornography or explicit media.
* Any other unwelcome physical, verbal, or non-verbal conduct of a sexual nature.

### 3. Hostile Environment & Quid Pro Quo (Section 3(2))
The following conduct when linked to sexual harassment acts constitutes a violation:
* **Preferential Treatment**: Promising high marks, grades, or promotions in exchange for sexual favors.
* **Detrimental Threats**: Threatening to withhold degrees, fail exams, or expel students if they refuse advances.
* **Hostile Environment**: Creating an intimidating, offensive, or hostile learning or hostel atmosphere.
* **Humiliation**: Subjecting the student to humiliating treatment likely to affect her health or safety.

### 4. Redressal Mechanism & Internal Committee (IC)
* **Mandatory Committee (Section 4)**: Every college, university, or workspace with 10 or more employees **must** establish an **Internal Committee (IC)** to investigate sexual harassment complaints.
* **Constitution of IC**:
  - **Presiding Officer**: Must be a woman employed at a senior level in the institution.
  - **Members**: At least two members committed to the cause of women or experienced in social work/legal knowledge.
  - **External Member**: One member from an NGO or association committed to the cause of women.
* **Filing Timeline (Section 9)**: A written complaint must be submitted to the IC within **3 months** of the incident (can be extended under special circumstances).
* **Inquiry Procedure**: The IC must complete its inquiry within **90 days**. The report must be submitted to the Head of Institution, who must take action within **60 days**.
