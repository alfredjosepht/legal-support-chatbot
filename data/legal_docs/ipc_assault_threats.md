# Indian Penal Code (IPC) - Physical Assault & Intimidation

### 1. Voluntarily Causing Hurt & Grievous Hurt
* **Voluntarily Causing Hurt (Section 323)**: Whoever does any act with the intention of causing bodily pain, disease, or infirmity to any person is guilty of causing hurt. Punishment is imprisonment up to 1 year, or a fine up to Rs. 1,000, or both.
* **Hurt by Dangerous Weapons (Section 324)**: Causing hurt by means of any instrument for shooting, stabbing, cutting, or any weapon. Punishable with imprisonment up to 3 years, or fine, or both.
* **Voluntarily Causing Grievous Hurt (Section 325)**: Grievous hurt includes permanent loss of sight/hearing, deprivation of any member or joint, fracture, or dislocation of a bone or tooth. Punishable with imprisonment up to 7 years and a fine.

### 2. Wrongful Restraint & Confinement
* **Wrongful Restraint (Section 339 / 341)**: Voluntarily obstructing a person so as to prevent that person from proceeding in any direction in which that person has a right to proceed. Punishable with simple imprisonment up to 1 month, or a fine up to Rs. 500, or both.
* **Wrongful Confinement (Section 340 / 342)**: Wrongfully restraining a person in such a manner as to prevent that person from proceeding beyond certain circumscribing limits. Punishable with imprisonment up to 1 year, or a fine up to Rs. 1,000, or both.

### 3. Criminal Intimidation & Verbal Abuse
* **Criminal Intimidation (Section 503 / 506)**: Threatening another with injury to their person, reputation, or property, with the intent to cause alarm or force them to act against their will.
  - *Standard Threat*: Punishable with imprisonment up to 2 years, or fine, or both.
  - *Grave Threat (to cause death or grievous hurt)*: Punishable with imprisonment up to 7 years, or fine, or both.
* **Insulting the Modesty of a Woman (Section 509)**: Uttering any word, making any sound or gesture, or exhibiting any object with intent to insult the modesty of a woman. Punishable with simple imprisonment up to 3 years and a fine.
