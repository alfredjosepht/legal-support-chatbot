# UGC (Promotion of Ragging Prevention) Regulations, 2009

### 1. Definition of Ragging
Under the UGC Regulations, ragging constitutes one or more of the following acts:
* **Rudeness & Teasing**: Any conduct by a student or students (spoken, written, or physical) that has the effect of teasing, treating, or handling with rudeness a fresher or any other student.
* **Undisciplined Activities**: Indulging in rowdy or undisciplined activities causing annoyance, hardship, physical/psychological harm, or raising fear or apprehension in a fresher.
* **Embarrassment & Shame**: Asking a student to perform acts they would not ordinarily do, generating a sense of shame, torment, or embarrassment.
* **Academic Disruption**: Acts by a senior student preventing, disrupting, or disturbing regular academic activities of other students.
* **Forced Labor/Exploitation**: Exploiting services of a fresher or student for completing academic tasks assigned to individuals or groups.
* **Extortion**: Financial extortion or putting a forceful expenditure burden on a fresher or other student.
* **Physical Abuse**: Any physical abuse, sexual abuse, homosexual assaults, stripping, forcing obscene/lewd acts, gestures, or causing bodily harm/health hazards.
* **Verbal & Digital Abuse**: Spoken words, emails, public insults, posts, or online messages derived from perverted or sadistic thrill to cause discomfort.
* **Mental Harassment**: Acts affecting the mental health and self-confidence of a fresher or other student.

### 2. Administrative Action in Institutions
Heads of Institutions must take immediate action upon receiving a ragging complaint:
* **Suspension**: Suspend the accused student from classes, academic privileges, or hostel.
* **Expulsion**: Expel the student from the institution or revoke their admission.
* **Withholding Results**: Withhold results, scholarships, or certificates.
* **Rustication**: Rusticate the student for a period ranging from one to four semesters.
* **Fines**: Impose financial penalties up to Rs. 2.5 Lakhs.

### 3. Redressal & Filing Procedure
* **Campus Level**: File a written complaint to the Anti-Ragging Committee, Head of Institution (Principal/Dean), or Hostel Warden immediately.
* **National Level Hotline**: Call the 24x7 National Anti-Ragging Helpline at **1800-180-5522** or email **helpline@antiragging.in**.
* **Anti-Ragging Portal**: Register a case online at [https://www.antiragging.in](https://www.antiragging.in).
* **Police Level**: File a formal FIR at the local police station under relevant sections of the Indian Penal Code (IPC) if the incident involves physical violence, confinement, or severe abuse.
