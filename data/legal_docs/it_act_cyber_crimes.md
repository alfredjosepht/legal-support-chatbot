# Information Technology (IT) Act, 2000 - Cyber Crimes

### 1. Identity Theft & Impersonation (Doxxing)
* **Identity Theft (Section 66C)**: Fraudulently or dishonestly making use of the password, digital signature, or any other unique identification feature of another person. Punishable with imprisonment up to 3 years and a fine up to Rs. 1 Lakh.
* **Cheating by Impersonation (Section 66D)**: Cheating by impersonating another person using any communication device or computer resource (e.g. creating fake social media profiles or email accounts). Punishable with imprisonment up to 3 years and a fine up to Rs. 1 Lakh.

### 2. Violation of Privacy & Cyber Harassment
* **Violation of Privacy (Section 66E)**: Intentionally capturing, publishing, or transmitting images of a person's private parts without their consent (e.g. voyeurism, hidden camera recordings, or sharing private photos). Punishable with imprisonment up to 3 years, a fine up to Rs. 2 Lakhs, or both.
* **Transmitting Obscene Material (Section 67)**: Publishing or transmitting in electronic form material that is lascivious or appeals to prurient interests (e.g. sharing vulgar messages or photos). First conviction: imprisonment up to 3 years and a fine up to Rs. 5 Lakhs.
* **Transmitting Sexually Explicit Material (Section 67A)**: Publishing or transmitting material containing sexually explicit acts in electronic form (e.g. revenge porn or explicit leaks). First conviction: imprisonment up to 5 years and a fine up to Rs. 10 Lakhs.

### 3. Procedure for Reporting Cyber Crimes
* **National Cyber Crime Portal**: Report incidents online anonymously or formally at [https://www.cybercrime.gov.in](https://www.cybercrime.gov.in).
* **Cyber Crime Cell**: Visit the nearest District Police Headquarters Cyber Crime Cell to lodge a written complaint.
* **Preservation of Evidence**:
  - Keep screenshots of abusive chats, profile links, and headers of threatening emails.
  - Do not delete log reports or chat histories, as they contain metadata needed for police investigations.
