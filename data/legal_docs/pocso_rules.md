# Protection of Children from Sexual Offences Act, 2012 (POCSO)

### 1. Who is Protected?
* **Definition of Child**: Under Section 2(1)(d), a "child" is any person below the age of **18 years**. The Act applies gender-neutrally to protect both boys and girls.

### 2. Categorization of Offences & Punishments
* **Penetrative Sexual Assault (Section 3)**: Penetrating the body of a child (vagina, mouth, urethra, or anus) with penis, fingers, or any object, or making the child do so with another person. Punishable under Section 4 with rigorous imprisonment of 10 to 20 years or life.
* **Aggravated Penetrative Sexual Assault (Section 5)**: Penetrative assault committed by a person in authority (relative, guardian, police officer, or **educational institution teacher/management/warden/staff**), or if committed during a disaster, or on a disabled child. Punishable under Section 6 with rigorous imprisonment of 20 years to life, or death penalty.
* **Sexual Assault (Section 7)**: Touching the child's body (breasts, vagina, penis, anus, etc.) with sexual intent, or forcing the child to touch others. Punishable under Section 8 with imprisonment of 3 to 5 years.
* **Aggravated Sexual Assault (Section 9)**: Sexual assault committed by relatives, police, **teachers, principal, or staff of an educational institution**, or causing bodily harm. Punishable under Section 10 with imprisonment of 5 to 7 years.
* **Sexual Harassment (Section 11)**: Showing pornography, making sexual gestures, stalking, following, or uttering obscene words/sounds to the child. Punishable under Section 12 with imprisonment up to 3 years.

### 3. Special Protections & Mandatory Reporting
* **Mandatory Reporting (Section 19)**: Any person (including school administrators, teachers, doctors, or citizens) who has knowledge or suspicion of a sexual offence against a child **must report** the incident.
* **Failure to Report (Section 21)**: Any institutional head or staff failing to report an offence is punishable with imprisonment up to 1 year.
* **Victim Anonymity (Section 23)**: It is a severe criminal offence to publish the child's name, address, school, photos, or any details that disclose the child's identity.
* **Child-Friendly Investigation**:
  - The police officer recording the statement must not wear a uniform.
  - The statement must be recorded at the child's residence or a place of their choice.
  - The child must not be kept at the police station overnight.
  - Female police officers must be involved in recording the child's statement.
  - No face-to-face contact with the accused is allowed during trial.
